About SwiftPDF

A small set of PDF tools that run entirely in your browser.

Why we built it

Most online converters upload your files to a server before doing anything. SwiftPDF processes files on your own device, inside your browser. File contents are not uploaded to SwiftPDF, so conversions happen without an upload or download round-trip.

How it works

Pick a tool, drop in your files, and download the result. Images can be cropped, reordered, and combined into a PDF. PDFs can be merged, compressed, converted to images, organized, split, extracted, and numbered in your browser.

One honest limitation

Converting Word or PowerPoint files to PDF can't be done inside a browser — it needs a conversion server. That's on the roadmap; for now, export those documents to PDF first and use the merge tool.

What's next

We keep improving the existing tools and adding features based on user feedback. Office-file conversion needs a server, so for now export Word or PowerPoint documents to PDF first. Got a tool you'd find useful? Tell us.
