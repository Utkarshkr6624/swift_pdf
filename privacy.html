Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

1. Overview

SwiftPDF provides browser-based PDF tools such as Image to PDF, PDF to Image, Merge PDF and Compress PDF. We designed the core tools so that file processing happens in your browser rather than requiring you to upload the files to a SwiftPDF server.

2. Files you process

For the browser-based tools, your selected files are processed locally by JavaScript running in your browser. SwiftPDF does not intentionally receive or store the contents of those files on a SwiftPDF server. Temporary browser memory, cached resources or browser storage may be used by your device while a tool is operating.

3. Website and hosting information

When you visit SwiftPDF, our hosting and infrastructure providers may automatically process limited technical information needed to deliver the website, such as IP address, browser/device information, requested pages, timestamps and security-related logs. This information is used for delivery, security, reliability and troubleshooting.

4. Contact messages

If you email SwiftPDF, we receive the information you choose to include in your message, such as your email address, subject and message contents. We use that information to respond to your request, investigate bugs and handle support or feedback. We do not sell your contact information.

5. Cookies, advertising and third-party services

SwiftPDF may use third-party services for website functionality, measurement or advertising. If advertising is enabled, advertising providers such as Google may use cookies or similar technologies to provide, measure and personalize ads as permitted by their policies and applicable law.

Third-party providers may collect information directly from your browser according to their own privacy policies. You should review the privacy policies of those providers for more information about their practices.

6. Google advertising

If Google AdSense or another Google advertising service is enabled on SwiftPDF, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure advertising. Where required, SwiftPDF will provide the applicable consent and privacy controls before or when those technologies are used.

You can learn more about Google's advertising practices and controls through Google's own privacy and advertising resources.

7. Children's privacy

SwiftPDF is a general-purpose utility website and is not specifically directed to children. We do not knowingly request personal information from children.

8. Data retention and security

Because the core PDF processing is performed in the browser, SwiftPDF is not designed to maintain a copy of your uploaded files on our servers. Information sent to us by email may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to the request, provide support, maintain records or resolve disputes.

9. Your choices

You can choose not to use a tool or send us personal information. You can also control cookies through your browser and, where applicable, through consent or advertising controls presented on the website.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when SwiftPDF's features, services or legal requirements change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised date.
