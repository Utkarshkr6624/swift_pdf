Types of complaints we handle

  • Content that appears to be illegal, or that infringes someone's copyright, privacy or other rights.
  • Privacy or data questions, including anything covered by the Privacy Policy.
  • Advertising problems, including inappropriate or misleading Google AdSense ads.
  • General bugs and feature requests for the tools.

If your complaint is about something on the site, please include the specific page URL and a short description of what is wrong. That is usually all we need to find it and act on it.

If you are not satisfied

Please complain to the Grievance Officer first, and give us up to 15 days to respond and resolve it. If the outcome does not satisfy you, you can lodge a grievance with the Data Protection Board of India under Section 80 of the Information Technology Act, 2000. Details are on the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology data protection framework page.

You can also report the matter to the relevant authorities, such as the police or a cyber cell, as per applicable law. Using this process does not stop you from doing that, and you do not have to come to us first.

What to include

To help faster, mention the browser you're using and, if it's a bug, what you were doing when it happened. Please don't attach the file itself unless we ask — describe it instead. Feature requests are just as welcome; the next tools get built from what you ask for.

What happens to your message

The address above is used only to reply to you and to keep a note of the problem, if there is one. It is not added to a mailing list, sold, or passed to anyone else, and you can ask for it to be deleted at any time. There is no contact form on this site, so nothing is collected before you decide to send something.

More detail is in the Privacy Policy. Complaints about content on the site, and privacy or takedown requests, go to the Grievance Officer above.
