Questions people ask

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. Every tool runs inside your browser using JavaScript. Your images and PDFs never leave your device — you could even disconnect from the internet and most tools would still work.

Is SwiftPDF free?

Yes, completely free, with no accounts, no watermarks and no limits on how many files you convert.

How do I convert JPG images to one PDF?

Open the Image to PDF tool, drop in your images, arrange their order in the edit view, and click Convert to PDF. The whole process takes a few seconds.

Can I merge Word or PowerPoint files?

Not yet — converting Office documents needs a server. Export them to PDF first (File → Save as PDF in Word or PowerPoint), then merge them here.
