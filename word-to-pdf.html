Word to PDF Converter

Convert between PDF, Word, Excel and PowerPoint on this device — a Word .docx into a PDF and the other eleven directions as well. Every result is rebuilt from the words, tables and pictures inside the file, not from its design. Nothing is uploaded.

The words, tables and pictures are copied into a new PDF set in a standard font. The page size, margins and page breaks are new, and Word's fonts, colours, columns, headers, footnotes and text boxes are not carried over.

One file at a time. The result is rebuilt text and tables — not the original fonts, colours or layout.

Compressing re-renders every page as an image to shrink the file. The result looks the same, but the text is no longer selectable or searchable.

What this conversion does not keep

    Every conversion this page makes

    A format is never converted into itself, so there are twelve. Each one keeps the words, tables and pictures it can read and rebuilds them; none of them keeps the original fonts, colours, page design or page breaks.

    • PDF (.pdf) → Word (.docx), Excel (.xlsx) or PowerPoint (.pptx)
    • Word (.docx) → PDF (.pdf), Excel (.xlsx) or PowerPoint (.pptx)
    • Excel (.xlsx) → PDF (.pdf), Word (.docx) or PowerPoint (.pptx)
    • PowerPoint (.pptx) → PDF (.pdf), Word (.docx) or Excel (.xlsx)

    Older .doc, .ppt and .xls files, macro-enabled documents and files that ask for a password cannot be opened here — open them in their own app and save a copy in the newer format first. One file is converted at a time. Nothing is uploaded: your file never leaves this device, and the conversion engine is fetched from the internet the first time you use it.
